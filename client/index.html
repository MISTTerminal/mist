MIST gives every position its own wallet and hides the thread between them, so the chain sees trades and never the trader.

What makes it private

Four layers between you and the watchers.

01

Compartments

Each token you buy lives in a fresh wallet, derived in your browser from a single seed. One position being found tells nobody about the next.

02

The mist route

Compartments are paid by a cross-chain route from its own wallet. No explorer shows your Reserve, or your main wallet, paying them.

03

Shield

Orders go straight to block builders in a private lane. They are never shown to the bots that sandwich public orders.

04

Scatter

Split a buy into random slices over a time window, so size and timing stop looking like one trader.

The relay

Your browser speaks to one server. It remembers nothing.

Charts, quotes, token pictures and transactions all pass through the MIST relay, so data providers and RPC nodes never see your address or what you are watching. There are no accounts, no cookies and no request log, and the page is locked by policy from loading anything off-site.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

What is MIST?

A trading terminal for Solana tokens built around privacy. It has what a terminal needs (trending, new pairs, live charts, a trade tape, one-click buys and sells) and adds a vault of compartment wallets, a private funding route, a private order lane and scattered orders.

Who holds my keys?

You do. A 32-byte seed is generated in your tab, encrypted with your passphrase (PBKDF2 with 310,000 rounds, then AES-256-GCM) and stored only in your browser. Every compartment key is derived from it. Transactions are signed in the tab with Ed25519 and reach the relay already signed. The recovery key rebuilds every compartment on any device, and each compartment key can be exported to any Solana wallet.

How does the mist route break the link between wallets?

The route is NEAR Intents. You deposit to a one-time address, on Solana or on another chain, and a solver pays the destination wallet in SOL from the solver's own wallet. On Solana the compartment's history starts with a payment from the route, not from you. Moving SOL between two of your own compartments works the same way.

What can still be seen?

Everything a compartment does on Solana is public, as with any wallet. What is missing is the thread from one compartment to another and to the wallet that funded them. Amounts and timing can still be compared by a determined observer, which is what Scatter and funding from another chain are for. A direct deposit to a compartment address is visible as a payment from the sender; the terminal labels those compartments so you always know which is which.

What does Shield do?

With Shield on, the signed order is handed to Jito block builders as a private bundle with a small tip, and it only lands if it lands whole. It is not broadcast where sandwich bots watch. If the private lane cannot take the order, the terminal tells you and does not quietly send it the public way.

Which chains can I fund from?

Any chain the route supports, including Solana, Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, BNB Chain, Bitcoin, Zcash, NEAR, Tron, Sui, XRP and more, in SOL, USDC, USDT, ETH, BTC and other majors. You always receive SOL in the compartment you chose.

What does the relay know about me?

Nothing it keeps. It has no accounts and sets no cookies. It writes no request log. Rate limiting uses a salted hash that is thrown away every minute and re-salted on every restart. The source is open so this can be checked.

What does it cost?

Network fees, the private-lane tip (0.0001 SOL per order by default) and the route's own spread on transfers. A new token account on Solana holds a 0.002 SOL deposit that the network returns when the account is closed.

What are the risks?

Tokens on Solana are volatile and many go to zero. If you lose both your passphrase and your recovery key, nobody can recover the vault. Clearing your browser data removes the vault from that browser, so keep the recovery key somewhere safe before you deposit.
